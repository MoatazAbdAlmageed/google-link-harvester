# Privacy Policy: Google Link Harvester

**Last Updated:** May 6, 2026

## **Overview**
Google Link Harvester ("the Extension") is a productivity tool designed to help users extract and organize organic search result links from Google Search. Your privacy is our priority, and this policy outlines how we handle data.

---

## **1. Data Collection**
The Extension **does not collect** any personally identifiable information (PII). We do not collect your name, email address, physical address, or any other personal data.

The only data the extension interacts with is the **content of the search result pages** you explicitly choose to harvest. This includes:
*   Hyperlinks (URLs)
*   Page Titles
*   Search Page numbers

---

## **2. Data Storage**
All harvested links and user preferences are stored **locally** on your device using the browser's storage API. We do not maintain any central servers, and your data is never uploaded to the cloud by the extension.

---

## **3. Data Usage**
The data collected is used solely for the single purpose of providing the link harvesting and export features to the user. We do not use your data for marketing, advertising, or profiling.

---

## **4. Third-Party Access**
We do not share, sell, or trade your data with third parties. Since your data is stored locally on your device, it remains under your exclusive control.

---

## **5. Permissions Justification**
The extension requires certain permissions (Scripting, Tabs, Storage) to function. These are used strictly to navigate between search pages and extract the links you request. We do not use these permissions to track your activity on other websites.

---

## **6. Changes to This Policy**
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
